Why I made this.

I work on a lot of creative projects, but I've never enjoyed documenting them. Opening DaVinci Resolve or Blender just to trim a devlog felt like overkill, and I spent more time fighting the software than actually editing and getting the update out. So I started leaving screen recording on and, at the end, asking Claude to stitch it all into a video and make the cuts for me. The results were good, but I couldn't fine-tune the edits the way I wanted. That's why I made ClipChop: intuitive, easy to pick up, no bloat. Download it, install it, and start editing.
